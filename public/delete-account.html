Delete Your Account & Data

This page explains how to permanently delete your MFM Lekki account and the personal data associated with it.

1 Delete from within the app (instant)

If you still have the MFM Lekki app installed, you can delete your account yourself at any time:

  1. Open the MFM Lekki app and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete My Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account is removed immediately.

2 Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, or are unable to sign in, send us a deletion request and we will process it for you.

Please send the email from the same address used for your MFM Lekki account so we can verify your identity.

Email a deletion request

Or email us directly at support@mfmlekkiphaseone.org. Requests are processed within 7 days.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

Account & profile Your name, email address, phone number, profile photo and login credentials.
Your activity Prayer requests, testimonies, social posts, chat messages and saved notes tied to your account are removed or permanently disassociated from you.
Device data Push-notification tokens linked to your account.

Retention: Account data is deleted immediately when you use the in-app option, or within 7 days of an emailed request. Limited records may be retained only where required by law (for example, financial or donation records), after which they are also deleted. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Need help?

Contact us at support@mfmlekkiphaseone.org and we will be glad to assist with your account or data request.
